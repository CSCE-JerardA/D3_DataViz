US Vehicle Registrations and Licensed Drivers (1960-2023)

These visualizations portray the Department of Transportation's data on licensed drivers and vehicle registrations in the US from 1960 to 2023. This dataset can be accessed at data.gov

Scatter Plot: Drivers vs Vehicles

This displays the relationship between the number of licensed drivers and the number of registered vehicles[cite: 1]. Red circles represent years where drivers outnumbered vehicles; blue circles represent years where drivers were less than or equal to vehicles[cite: 1]

Bar Chart: Vehicle Registrations Over Time

This bar chart displays the total number of registered vehicles for each year from 1960 through 2023[cite: 1]. Hover over any bar to highlight it and view details[cite: 1].
